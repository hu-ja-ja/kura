## [0.1.0](https://github.com/hu-ja-ja/kura/releases/tag/v0.1.0) - 2026-11-06

First release: a native Windows Bitwarden client.

### Added
- Sign in with master password, TOTP and email two-factor; saved login unlock
- Vault list, search, folders, favorites, details and copy
- Tray, auto-lock, autostart and screen capture protection
- Signed updates from inside the app
